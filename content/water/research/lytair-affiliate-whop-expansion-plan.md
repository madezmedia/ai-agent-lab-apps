## Executive Read

The cleanest path is to use Lytair / Off-Grid Water Vault as the prototype for a repeatable offer lane:

`faceless content -> bridge page -> advertorial/buyer guide -> email capture -> affiliate/Whop checkout -> ACMI event evidence`

Lytair should not start with dropshipping. Top recommendation: build the audience and buyer-intent surface first, then decide which commerce rails deserve operational commitment.

## Offer Architecture

### Lane A: Lytair Affiliate Content Engine

Purpose: rank, capture, and convert water-preparedness intent without inventory risk.

Core assets:

- Off-Grid Water Vault homepage section: clear category hub, not a generic blog.
- Buyer guides:
  - Best water filters for apartments, RVs, cabins, and emergency kits.
  - Rainwater collection starter guide.
  - Countertop filtration vs gravity filtration vs purification tablets.
  - Atmospheric water generators: what they are, when they make sense, and when they do not.
- Product pages:
  - Amazon-safe comparison pages with affiliate disclosure.
  - TikTok Shop product-watch pages once eligibility and product quality are verified.
  - Direct partner pages only after terms are received in writing.
- Email capture:
  - “7-Day Water Readiness Checklist”
  - “Off-Grid Water Buyer Matrix”
  - “Emergency Water Calculator”

Monetization stack:

- Amazon Associates: immediate low-friction catalog coverage; use for commodity products and search-intent pages.
- TikTok Shop affiliate: video-first testing once creator/account requirements are met; use for products with visual demo appeal.
- Direct AWG/dealer/referral: slow-burn high-ticket lane; requires partner vetting, compliance review, warranty/shipping/service clarity.
- Whop-owned digital product: best margin and easiest affiliate recruitment if packaged well.

### Lane B: Whop-Owned Water Vault

Purpose: stop being only an affiliate middle layer. Build a paid owned product that affiliates can promote.

Recommended first Whop:

`Off-Grid Water Vault: Starter Kit`

Possible contents:

- Water readiness checklist.
- Product decision matrix.
- Setup calculators.
- Monthly product-watch updates.
- Regional risk prompts.
- Emergency storage plan templates.
- “Don’t buy this unless…” AWG decision guide.

Pricing hypothesis:

- Starter: $9 to $19 one-time.
- Pro: $29 to $49 one-time or low monthly if updates/community justify it.
- Affiliate commission: start at 30% only if margin allows; confirm dashboard economics before finalizing.

Why Whop here:

- Built-in checkout and affiliate infrastructure.
- Can recruit creator affiliates around a digital bundle rather than asking them to promote low-commission Amazon items.
- Creates owned revenue even when Amazon/TikTok terms change.

### Lane C: AWG Partner Vetting

Purpose: identify whether atmospheric water generators are an affiliate, distributor, or lead-gen business.

Do not dropship AWGs yet. Open questions are too large:

- Unit economics unknown.
- Warranty liability unknown.
- Shipping/install/service unknown.
- Product performance depends on humidity, power, and environment.
- Claims risk is high if content implies universal water independence.

Vet partners in this order:

1. Public partner/referral programs with visible terms.
2. Manufacturers with distributor pages and sales enablement.
3. Retailers with established affiliate networks.
4. Dropship suppliers only after warranty and support liability is explicit.

Decision rule:

- If payout is under 5% and support burden is high, keep AWG as content/lead-gen only.
- If payout is 8% to 15% with no support liability, test as affiliate.
- If distributor margin is strong but requires inventory/service, defer until Lytair has traffic and leads.
