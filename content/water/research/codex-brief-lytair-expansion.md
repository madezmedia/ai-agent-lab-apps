## Research questions, one section each in the deliverable

### 1. Amazon Associates
- Program rules that bite:
  - the 180-day/3-sale qualification;
  - no Associates links in email or PDFs;
  - no hardcoded prices without the PA-API;
  - the required disclosure wording;
  - the rules on links in social posts.
- Which water-storage products fit an honest lytair guide: storage barrels, bricks, filters, bleach/test strips, portable atmospheric water generators (AWGs). Gather real category commission rates from Amazon's current rate table and cite them.
- The site structure needed: comparison and guide pages that pass Associates review.

### 2. TikTok Shop affiliate (creator)
- Eligibility: follower minimums, region, business vs creator account.
- Product categories available: water filtration, storage, AWGs.
- Commission ranges, citing sources.
- Content rules: health and safety claim limits, and the AI-generated content labels.
- Whether a faceless account can qualify.

### 3. Atmospheric water generators: affiliate vs dropship
- Affiliate programs from real AWG brands and retailers (residential and portable units): commission, cookie length, and their approval and claims rules. Cite each program page.
- **Dropship feasibility:**
  - supplier vetting: US warehouse, UL/ETL/FCC certification, NSF/ANSI 53/58/42 claims, warranty, returns on 50–150 lb freight, DOA rate;
  - payment-processor and marketplace risk;
  - product-liability insurance;
  - sales tax;
  - margin after freight and returns.
- Recommend affiliate-first, dropship-first or skip, with the numbers.
- **Claims guardrails:** EPA/FTC guidance on water-from-air output and "safe to drink" claims; humidity and temperature limits on output; energy cost per liter. Every output claim on our pages must be attributed and conditional (e.g. "at 80°F / 60% RH, per the manufacturer").

### 4. Advertorials and blog
- Advertorial format that pop and native networks accept for this niche (Traffics.io, PropellerAds, Taboola/Outbrain policies), with the required "Advertisement" labeling.
- A blog content plan for lytair.com:
  - 20 topics ranked by search demand and buyer intent, with keyword data where available;
  - which posts monetize via Amazon, AWG affiliate or the Joseph's Well link;
  - the internal links to the PDF and quiz.
- Site-architecture recommendation (current static Vercel site vs adding a CMS), with the trade-offs.

### 5. Faceless social and YouTube
- Channel plan for YouTube (long form plus Shorts), TikTok, Instagram Reels, Pinterest and Facebook:
  - formats: explainers, storage how-tos, product teardowns and comparisons, "what the numbers really mean" claim checks;
  - cadence;
  - production pipeline (script → voice → B-roll/screen → captions);
  - tools.
- **Platform policies to meet:**
  - YouTube's reused/repetitive content and inauthentic-content monetization rules;
  - its altered/synthetic content disclosure;
  - the TikTok AI-generated content label;
  - FTC endorsement guides (affiliate disclosure in video and description).

- How the videos use lytair branding: lower thirds, end card, and a palette-matched thumbnail system based on the kit.
