### Post 002: AWG Reality Check

Asset id: `post-002-awg-reality`

Atmospheric water generators are interesting.

But “water from air” is not the same thing as “free water anywhere.”

Before anyone spends serious money on one, they should ask:

- What humidity does it need?
- What temperature range does it need?
- How much power does it use?
- Who handles warranty claims?
- What happens if a 50+ lb unit has to be returned?
- Is there a US fulfillment or service path?

For most households, storage + transfer + testing + basic filtration should come before a high-ticket AWG.

### Post 003: Rain Barrel Warning

Asset id: `post-003-rain-barrel`

A rain barrel can be useful.

But a rain barrel is not automatically a drinking-water plan.

Roof runoff can carry debris. Stored water can grow things. Local rules vary. Treatment matters. Testing matters.

That does not mean rain catchment is bad. It means it belongs in a system:

1. collect
2. store
3. move
4. test
5. treat/filter based on the actual use case

### Post 005: Stop Asking Best Filter

Asset id: `post-005-best-filter`

“What is the best water filter?” is usually the wrong first question.

Better questions:

- What problem are you solving?
- Is the water already treated tap water?
- Is it stored emergency water?
- Is it rainwater?
- Are you worried about sediment, taste, bacteria, chemicals, or something else?
- Do you need storage, transfer, or testing before filtration?

No single product is the whole plan.
